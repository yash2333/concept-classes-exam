Step 3 — Apply

📌 3. Key Points for Revision

  • Know the important definitions and terminology.
  • Remember the rules, formulae, diagrams or processes relevant to the chapter.
  • Understand the difference between similar-looking concepts.
  • Practise at least one basic and one application-based question.
  • Review mistakes instead of only checking the final answer.

💡 4. Worked-Example Method

Concept Classes Method

Read → Identify → Apply → Check → Explain

For numerical questions, write the given information, choose the correct formula or principle, substitute carefully, calculate, include units where required, and check whether the answer is reasonable.

⚠️ 5. Common Mistakes

  • Learning an answer without understanding the concept behind it.
  • Skipping important steps in numerical or logical answers.
  • Using the wrong unit, symbol, term or formula.
  • Ignoring the wording of the question.
  • Not checking the final answer before submitting.
